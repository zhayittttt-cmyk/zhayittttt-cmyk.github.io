Research

Gamma oscillations in the vestibular cortex

Summer 2026 · Undergraduate Researcher, Computational Systems Neuroscience Laboratory (Prof. Maurice Chacron, Department of Physiology, McGill) · Mackey-Glass Summer Research Bursary

Recordings from the parieto-insular vestibular cortex (PIVC) show stronger gamma-band activity (roughly 30–58 Hz) during natural vestibular stimulation than during artificial stimuli such as sinusoidal motion. I worked on a computational model of this difference: a recurrent network of exponential integrate-and-fire excitatory and inhibitory neurons, driven by both kinds of stimulus and compared against the experimental local field potential recordings.

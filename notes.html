Course notes

Notes I took during lectures at McGill; they are not official course material, and they will contain mistakes. If you find one or you'd like to have the handwritten manuscript, email me or open an issue.

Some material in these notes was not covered in the course; I added it for completeness — for instance, locally compact Hausdorff spaces in the MATH 592 notes and quadratic forms in the MATH 251 notes. If you're using them to study for a course, check its description in the McGill course catalogue for what is actually on the syllabus.

Courses marked “not shared” are ones I took whose notes I'm not posting. Contact me if you want notes for these courses.
